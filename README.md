# Bridge to Lanka: playable demo

A three-lane skate runner with Tony Hawk-style tricks, told as a Ramayan side story.
Shambu the langur missed the bus to Lanka, so he skates the bridge and takes on Surasa, the sea serpent.

By CuSiC Interactive (ArcadeWala), made in Godot 4.7.

## Play it

| Platform | Link |
|---|---|
| Android (7.0 and up) | [Download BridgeToLanka.apk](https://github.com/CuSiC-Interactive/BridgeToLanka-Demo/releases/latest/download/BridgeToLanka.apk) |
| iPhone | [Join the TestFlight beta](https://testflight.apple.com/join/QqpKkY9A) |

Both links always point to the newest build.

**Installing on Android:** open the download, and allow installs from your browser or Files app when Android asks. Play Protect may warn you because the app isn't from the Play Store; tap "Install anyway". A newer build installs over the old one.

**On iPhone:** install TestFlight from the App Store, then open the link above.

## What's in the demo

- A tutorial with the Skate Guru
- Three levels on the bridge to Lanka
- Surasa, a four-phase boss fight
- Endless mode
- The King's Stall, a cosmetics shop paid for with bananas you earn by skating

Notes for each build are on the [Releases](https://github.com/CuSiC-Interactive/BridgeToLanka-Demo/releases) page.
